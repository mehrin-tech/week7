After $group: Calculates the average GPA per major:

simple> db.students.aggregate([{$match:{enrollment_year:2022}},{$group:{_id:
"$major",averageGpa:{$avg:"$gpa"}}}])
[
  { _id: 'Physics', averageGpa: 3.4 },
  { _id: 'Computer Science', averageGpa: 3.92 }
]

After $project: The output documents are reshaped to:

simple> db.students.aggregate([{$match:{enrollment_year:2022}},{$group:{_id:
